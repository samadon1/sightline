# Hands-on UX and QA review, 3 September 2026

**Verdict: NO-GO for a judge demo as built. Two blockers sit on the two buttons a judge is most likely to press (Replay and Next scene); the caption rendering itself is in good shape.**

Scope: the installed `vega-player` build on the Vega Virtual Device, driven only through the remote keys. Every finding below has a capture. Captures live in the session scratchpad at `scratchpad/uxaudit/` (paths below are relative to that folder; `*-s.png` are half-scale crops of the device area, `<name>/NN.png` are one-per-second bursts, `*-sheet.png` are contact sheets). Settings were Speaker-aware, Reduced motion on, Word highlighting on, Size and weight on unless a finding says otherwise. The device was left on the landing page with Speaker-aware and Reduced motion on (`ZZ-final-state.png`, `PS04-settings.png`).

Counts: 2 blocker, 4 high, 16 medium, 13 low.

---

## Blocker

### B1. Replay leaves the "Scene finished" card on screen while the scene replays underneath

- Did: let the bridge finish. On the end card, with Replay focused, pressed Select. Captured at +1 s, +3 s, +7 s. Pressed Select again twice.
- Saw: the video restarted and captions played, but the end card stayed on top with Replay still focused (`E10-replay-1s.png`, `E11-replay-3s.png`, `E12-replay-7s.png`). Each further Select restarted the scene from 0 again (`E13-select.png`, `E14-select-again.png`). The card stayed for the full 48 s (`spk/01.png` to `spk/50.png`, an entire burst with the card on top of every caption). Reproduced three times (also `B0-after-enter.png`, `X17-right.png` to `X22-after-2s.png`). While stuck, there is no way to pause, the chrome cannot be shown, and Left/Right move focus on the card instead of seeking. Log: after `event=ended` the player emits `MediaPlayer: play` but no `play` event reaches the app (`[sync] event=play` appears only after a pause/resume, never after ended).
- Expected: Replay hides the card and returns to normal playback with the chrome.
- Smallest fix: in the Replay handler set `setPlay("playing")` optimistically (and `setPanel("controls")`), and also subscribe to the `playing` event as a fallback for `play`.

### B2. After "Next scene", no captions appear in Standard or Speaker-aware for the rest of the scene, on every scene

- Did: bridge to end card, Next scene, captured at +1.5 s and +4 s, seeked to the end, repeated through all ten scenes (Speaker-aware).
- Saw: no caption at any sampled time on the lab (~4 s, cue c002 active, `NS02-b.png`), The Envelope (5.5 s, `NS03-b.png`), Keys (1.6 s, `NS04-a.png`), Hallway (4.3 s, `NS05-b.png`), Clock test (`NS06-b.png`), and every fixture (`NS07-b.png` to `NS10-b.png`). The same scenes played directly from the landing show captions at once (`keysdirect/03.png`, `labspk/03.png`, `tsspk5/02.png`). In Detailed the overlay does survive Next scene (`R1-11-next-6s.png`, `R1-12-next-9s.png`), so the loss is specific to the TV-drawn caption view after a new `VideoPlayer` is created inside the still-mounted `PlayerScreen`.
- Expected: the second scene captions exactly like the first.
- Smallest fix: after `await p.initialize()` in the scene effect, re-attach the existing view: `if (captionHandleRef.current) p.setCaptionViewHandle(captionHandleRef.current)`; or key `PlayerScreen` on `scene.id` in `App.tsx` so a scene change remounts the player.

## High

### H1. Settings do not survive a relaunch (three of three tries)

- Did: set Detailed (landing pill read "Captions · Detailed", `R2-00-lab-selected.png`), relaunched with `vega run-app`, waited 9 s. Later set Standard (`R2-08-landing.png`) and relaunched. Finally set Detailed and Reduced motion off (`PS01-set-detailed-rm-off.png`, `PS02-landing.png`), relaunched, waited 14 s.
- Saw: after every relaunch the pill read "Captions · Speaker-aware" and the settings screen showed Speaker-aware selected with Reduced motion on, i.e. the defaults (`R2-04-relaunched.png`, `R2-09-relaunched-persist.png`, `PS03-relaunched.png`, `PS04-settings.png`).
- Expected: the stored preferences come back.
- Caveat: `vega run-app <vpkg>` is the only relaunch path on the virtual device and may reinstall the package, which would clear app storage; the JS log of the relaunched process was not captured by the log stream, so there is no `[settings] restored` line either way. Treat as unverified rather than proven broken, but it needs a launcher relaunch on hardware before the demo.
- Smallest fix: confirm with a launcher relaunch on a Fire TV stick; if `run-app` is the cause, note it in the runbook; if not, check that `AsyncStorage.setItem` resolves on Vega (log the promise result) and hydrate before first paint.

### H2. The remote's Play/Pause and Fast-forward keys do nothing in the player

- Did: pressed KEY_PLAYPAUSE and KEY_FASTFORWARD on the landing and in the player.
- Saw: no change (`X13-ff.png`, `X14-playpause.png`); the log records `[remote] unmapped event "play"` and `[remote] unmapped event "forward"` each time. The map in `useRemote.ts` expects `playpause`, `fast_forward`, `skip_forward`, which the device does not send. KEY_REWIND does work (mapped as `rewind`) but jumps to 0:00 silently, with no chrome and no hint (`B0-after-rewind.png`).
- Expected: Play/Pause toggles like Select; Fast-forward seeks; both show the chrome.
- Smallest fix: add `play`, `pause`, `forward`, `backward` to `MAP`; call `setPanel("controls")` on skipBack/skipForward; add a hint pill for the skip keys.

### H3. Detailed captions are about half the height of the TV's own captions at the same system size

- Did: compared the same lines in Speaker-aware (TV renderer) and Detailed (overlay).
- Saw: "Look Celia, we have to follow our passions;" is roughly 55 px cap height natively and under 30 px in Detailed at half scale (`zoom-det.png`, top-right vs bottom-left; `labspk/03.png` vs `labdet2/03.png`). Quiet lines in the lower-left lane are smaller still (`bridgedet/08.png`). The mode a viewer chooses for more help gives them less legible text, and the plain fallback on unverified scenes (the whole lab) is also drawn small by the overlay rather than by the TV.
- Expected: a "normal" Detailed line matches the native normal line; loudness scales around that.
- Smallest fix: raise the Detailed base size (`type.caption` and the lane clamp in `layout.ts`) so a cue with no delivery data equals the measured native line height, then re-check the 35 percent box clamp.

### H4. About: the last section is clipped and cannot be reached

- Did: opened About; pressed Down, Right, Up, Left.
- Saw: "Prototype notes" is cut off at the bottom of the glass panel and no key scrolls it; only Back is focusable (`A11-about-big.png`, `A12-down.png`, `A13-right.png`). The About page is also plain black while the settings page sits on the scene backdrop, so the two "secondary" screens look like different apps (`A11-about.png` vs `S01-open.png`).
- Expected: everything on the page can be read from the remote.
- Smallest fix: trim the copy to fit, or make the panel focusable so Down scrolls it; give About the same backdrop as settings.

## Medium

### M1. Scene rail: the focused card is drawn clipped at the right edge; the scroll catches up one press late

- Did: Down from Play, then Right nine times.
- Saw: on each step the newly focused card sits half off the window edge with its focus ring cut, and only the following press scrolls it into view (`R05-right.png`, `R06-right.png`, `R07-right.png`, `R08-right.png`, `R09-right.png`; `R10-right.png` shows the last card finally in view). Moving left is fine (`R203-left.png`). Fast presses recover (`R1-01-rapid-right.png`).
- Expected: the focused card is always fully visible.
- Smallest fix: on card focus, `scrollTo` so the card plus one card's margin is inside the viewport, or add `paddingRight` equal to a card width.

### M2. Pill row and rail are only loosely connected; focus jumps to spatial neighbours

- Did: Right on About; Up from a card; Down from About.
- Saw: Right from About drops into the middle of the rail on "Keys" (`L03-right-again.png`); Up from The Envelope lands on About, not Play (`L06-up.png`); Down from About lands on "Test: no companion file", not the card last focused (`R13-down-again.png`).
- Expected: Right at the end of the row stays; Down goes to the current scene's card; Up returns to Play.
- Smallest fix: set `nextFocusRight` on About to itself and `nextFocusDown` on all three pills to the current card; `nextFocusUp` on cards to Play.

### M3. One-line native captions draw over the transport chrome

- Did: Select to show the chrome while a one-line caption is up.
- Saw: "Whatever, Thom. We're done." sits on top of the progress row and hint pills (`Y04-right.png`). Two-line captions just clear the chrome (`P06-select-pause.png`).
- Expected: chrome and captions never overlap.
- Smallest fix: while `panel === "controls"`, inset the `KeplerCaptionsView` bottom by the chrome height (or move the chrome into the top scrim).

### M4. The "♫ music ♫" label is up almost continuously and makes the dialogue jump a line each time it comes and goes

- Did: Detailed bursts on the lab and the bridge.
- Saw: the label is present in 40 of 62 lab frames and from 25 s to the end of the bridge (`labdet2-sheet.png`, `bridgedet-sheet.png`). Whenever it appears the dialogue box moves up one line and drops back when it goes (`labdet2/09.png` vs `labdet2/13.png`; `labdet2/47.png` to `labdet2/51.png`). With Reduced motion on this is still a layout jump.
- Expected: dialogue holds its position within a shot; a continuous score does not need a permanent label.
- Smallest fix: reserve the bottom lane for the sound label while any sound event is active in the shot (dialogue stays raised), and cap a continuous "music" label to a few seconds per onset.

### M5. Per-word size changes inside a line give a ragged baseline that reads as a glitch

- Did: Detailed burst on the bridge with Word highlighting on.
- Saw: "follow", "your robotics", "being" are visibly larger than their neighbours mid-line (`bridgewh/13.png`, `bridgewh/16.png` to `bridgewh/21.png`); "freaked" is bold and larger, which reads as intended emphasis (`bridgewh/34.png`). The settings preview only ever shows one enlarged word, so a viewer is not prepared for this.
- Expected: size follows the voice per line or per phrase, not per word, unless the delta is large.
- Smallest fix: quantise word size to the cue value unless `wordHeightPct` differs by more than a threshold; keep bold emphasis.

### M6. Stale-metadata test: cue 3 (unknown speaker id) keeps its side lane but loses its colour

- Did: played "Test: stale metadata" in both modes.
- Saw: cue 1 (stale hash) and cue 2 (proposed) fall back to a white bottom-centre caption as promised, but cue 3 renders white in the left lane in Speaker-aware (`tsspk7/08.png` to `tsspk7/10.png`) and in Detailed (`tsdet7/08.png` to `tsdet7/10.png`). The log confirms `[companion] error $.cues.c003.speaker: Unknown speaker id "ghost"`. Placement is an enhancement too, so this is a half fallback.
- Expected: an unknown speaker gives up placement as well as colour.
- Smallest fix: in the resolver, when `speaker_unavailable`, also clear the lane.

### M7. Captions sheet: a Down press sometimes moves two rows, and the last row sometimes needs two Downs to reach the footer

- Did: Down, Down, Select from Speaker-aware in the in-playback sheet; Down from the last row.
- Saw: once, two Downs went from Speaker-aware past Reduced motion to Word highlighting and Select toggled it off (`MD01-sheet-from-endcard.png` to `MD02-detailed.png`); the same presses another time landed on Reduced motion (`MS03-down.png`, `MS04-down.png`). - Smallest fix: give the list a fixed height that fits all rows (no ScrollView), or set explicit `nextFocusDown`/`nextFocusUp` between rows and to the footer.

### M8. The "Detailed" group is cut in half behind the footer in both the full screen and the sheet, with no scroll hint

## What worked (for the record)

Landing initial focus on Play every time, including after playback and after a relaunch (`N06-relaunched.png`, `LB01-back.png`); Left/Up at Play stay put; rapid presses never lose focus (`R1-01-rapid-right.png`, `R1-02-rapid-left.png`). Menu and Back during loading (`P02-menu-during-loading.png`, `P04-back-closes-sheet.png`). Chrome appears on Select, stays while paused, hides about 4 s after resume (`P06`, `P07`, `P09`). Seek clamps at 0:00 and at the end (`P12-left-at-start.png`, `Y06-right.png`). Back with chrome up exits cleanly (`Y02-back-with-chrome.png`). End-card focus walks Replay, Next scene, Captions, Back and clamps at both ends (`E-sheet.png`). Mode switch inside the sheet updates the caption on screen immediately (`X03-select-spk.png`, `X05-select-std.png`). "Use Standard captions" and Done/Resume close the sheet (`Y14-use-standard.png`, `S22-done.png`). Speaker-aware on the bridge: names in colour, words colouring as spoken, Thom's lines in the lower-left lane (`spk-sheet-a.png`). Detailed on the bridge with Word highlighting on: white read-ahead turning to the speaker's colour per word, italics on cues 1, 8 and 9, emphasis on "freaked", the lane logic and the two-line fold (`bridgewh-sheet-a.png`, `bridgewh-sheet-b.png`). Clock test and protected-region fixtures behave as their subtitles say in both modes (`tsspk5-sheet.png`, `tsdet8-sheet.png`). Ten rapid Selects, five Menu/Back pairs, keys in the first second, 60 s idle and Home all leave the player healthy (`R1-06`, `R1-08`, `R1-03`, `R2-02-idle-62s.png`, `R2-03-home-during-playback.png`). No `[crash]` line in 352k log lines.

## Not testable on the virtual device

- A real launcher relaunch (Home is inert on the VVD), so H1 stays unverified.
- The physical remote's key codes for Play/Pause, Rewind and Fast-forward, and key hold or repeat behaviour.
- System caption size, font and colour changes (no Accessibility settings page on the VVD), so the "when the line fits there at your caption size" promise in Speaker-aware.
- Audio, so sound-label timing against the actual sound.
- The packaged (offline) media and caption path: the LAN server answered every time (`[player] source=lan`, `[assets] source=remote`).
- The JS log of any process started after the first relaunch: the log stream stopped carrying `[KeplerScript-JavaScript]` lines for new processes.
- Performance and thermals on a Fire TV stick.
