# Information sheet and consent: TV caption prototype test

Version 1.0, 2026-09-02. Plain language. This is product testing, not medical research.

## Information sheet

**What this is.** I am Samuel, an independent developer. I am building a prototype that shows captions on a TV in new ways, including labels that say who is speaking. I want to know whether the new ways help you follow who is talking, and whether you like them, compared with normal captions.

**What happens.** You watch three short versions of the same scene on a TV or screen. Each version uses a different caption style. After each one I ask a few questions about what you saw, and ask you to rate how easy it was to read and how distracting it was. At the end you tell me which style you prefer and why. There are no right or wrong answers; we are testing the captions, not you.

**How long.** 45 to 60 minutes (about 30 minutes for a pilot session). We can take breaks.

**Language and access.** You can have a sign language interpreter, live captions on the call, or a written-chat session. Tell me what you need and I will arrange it.

**Recording.** Recording is optional. If you say yes, I record the call so I can check my notes. Only I see the recording. You can say no and the session works the same.

**How I use what you say.** I may use short quotes from you in a project write-up and a hackathon submission. Quotes never include your name, face, voice, city, or anything that could identify you. If you asked, I will show you quotes before use.

**Payment.** 60 USD for a full session or 35 USD for a pilot session, paid within 48 hours by the method you chose. If you stop early you are still paid.

**Stopping.** You can stop at any time and you do not need to give a reason. You can ask me to delete your data at any time up to 2026-12-31.

**Your data.** I keep your contact details, your screener answers, my notes, and any recording in a private folder that only I can open. I do not share them with anyone. I delete contact details and recordings by 2026-12-31. Anonymous notes and scores may be kept longer.

**Contact.** Samuel, [email] and [messaging app]. Ask anything before, during, or after the session.
