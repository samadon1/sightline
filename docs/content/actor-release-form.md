### 2. What the Producer may do with it

I give the Producer a **perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, irrevocable licence** to use my Performance, in whole or in part, in any medium now known or invented later, including:

- in the Film and any edits, cuts, versions, and derivative works of it;
- in a **public source code repository** (including on services such as GitHub) where the Film and its caption files are stored alongside software;
- in **demo videos, screenshots, presentations, research papers, and study materials** that show the software working with the Film;
- in **user studies and research** where participants watch the Film;
- for promotion of the Film, the software, and the research.

The Producer may edit, cut, caption, dub, describe, and reformat my Performance, and may combine it with other material. I understand my Performance may be shown next to captions that name my character and describe sounds.

### 3. Open licence (Creative Commons)

I understand and agree that the Producer intends to **publish the Film under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)**, or a similar open licence, and may do so at any time.

I understand what that means in plain terms:

- Anyone in the world may copy, share, adapt, remix, and reuse the Film, including for commercial purposes, as long as they credit the Producer as the licence requires.
- This includes my Performance as it appears in the Film.
- Once the Film is published under an open licence, that publication **cannot be taken back**, and the Producer cannot control what other people make from it.

I also understand that the software next to the Film is released under the MIT licence. The MIT licence covers the code. The Film and my Performance are covered by CC BY 4.0 (or similar) and by this release.

### 4. Credit

The Producer will make reasonable efforts to credit me by the name I wrote above in the Film's credits and in the repository's credits file. If a credit is missed or dropped in a copy made by someone else, that is not a breach of this agreement.

### 5. Approval and payment

I do not have a right to inspect or approve how the Film or my Performance is used. I will not be paid royalties or any further amount for any use. Any payment or expenses for the shoot are set out here: ______________________________________ (write "none" if none).

### 6. My rights in the Performance

To the extent the law allows, I assign to the Producer any copyright or performers' rights I may have in the recordings of my Performance. Where those rights cannot be assigned, I grant the licence in section 2 instead. To the extent the law allows, I waive any moral rights or similar rights in the Performance so that the Film can be edited and published as described above.

### 7. My promises

- I am free to sign this and no other agreement stops me.
- I will not bring branded clothing, props, music, or other people's copyrighted material to the shoot without telling the Producer.
- My Performance is my own and does not copy someone else's protected work.

### 8. Personal data

The Producer will keep this signed form and my contact details only to prove that I gave permission, to credit me, and to contact me about the Film. They will not be published. My image and voice as they appear in the Film will be public, as described above.

### 9. The Producer's promises

- The Producer will not use my Performance in a way that is defamatory, pornographic, or that falsely suggests I endorse a specific product or political position.
- The Producer will use my Performance only as part of the Film, related material, and the research and software described in this form.

### 10. General

This is the whole agreement about my Performance in the Film. It can only be changed in writing signed by both of us. If part of it is unenforceable, the rest still applies. It is governed by the law of ______________________________________ (country or state).
